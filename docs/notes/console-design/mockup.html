1 · The console at 1280 × 800

The minimum supported viewport, at size. Twelve rows fit without scrolling — that is the constraint the whole layout is built around, because a panel that scrolls at twelve is a panel where the vehicle needing attention is off screen.

The fleet flies the circuit the station's own feed flies: a 400 m circle about the Huntsville origin, one lap in 120 s, twelve vehicles evenly spaced, all at 20.9 m/s. Every heading is tangent to that circle, so if a dart is not lying along the route line the arithmetic is wrong and you can see it without reading a number. UAV-07 is lost from the start; cut UAV-03's link to watch one go stale, and recall UAV-09 to see the return-to-launch state the console does not build yet.

3 · Luminance does the work

Each state is roughly half the luminance of the one above it — 0.49×, then 0.49×. That fell out of choosing the hues rather than being imposed on them, and it is what makes the language survive greyscale, colour blindness and a bad monitor at once: in every one of those the hue degrades and the brightness does not. Luminance encodes confidence in the data, not severity — which is why the lost vehicle is the dimmest thing on the map even when it is the most alarming.

A drawing, not a component. Nothing here is imported by the console and nothing here is a design system — when the states are built they are built from the note, in the console's own stack, and this page stops being the reference.

It reaches no origin but its own, same as the thing it describes: no webfont, no CDN, every mark drawn in place. Open it with file:// or any static server; it has no build step. Its two extracted specimens live beside it as states.svg and age-chip.svg.

The circuit, speed and origin mirrored the hardcoded feed the station flew when this was drawn. That feed has since been deleted and the station flies a MAVLink aircraft on a waypoint route instead, so these numbers are now illustrative rather than a copy of anything shipping — the drawing is about the state language, not the flight path.
